How I travel

Three rules run every trip on this site — and every itinerary I hand to a friend.

🍽️Plates first

Find where the local community eats, especially the Indian food that's cooked for Indian palates — not the watered-down version.

✈️Planes always

Layovers are spotting sessions. Heavies, special liveries, sail-aways — if it moves and it's big, I'm watching it leave.

🚫Skip the traps

If a place is famous mostly with tourists, it's probably not where dinner is. Locals first, lines of visitors last.

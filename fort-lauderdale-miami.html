The Miami day trip · Oct 2

Little Havana first

Cab down from Fort Lauderdale and straight to Sanguich de Miami in Little Havana — a proper Cuban sandwich before a stroll down Calle Ocho. That's the correct order of operations in Miami: eat first, wander second.

South Beach

The bar I lasted ten seconds in

Staff at the Le Labo store pointed me to Mac's Club Deuce, a Miami Beach institution. Walked in, smelled the smoke, walked straight back out. Some landmarks you experience from the sidewalk. Art Deco walk and beach time instead — no regrets.

Standing rule: if a bar smells like smoke, I'm out — doesn't matter how famous it is.

South Pointe Park · Evening

Sail-away hour

The day ended at South Pointe Park, at the jetty where the cruise ships slide out of PortMiami. I was almost talked out of the detour — glad I went anyway. MSC Seaside was already sailing in the distance, Wonder of the Seas and Norwegian Getaway had gone before her, and two more ships sat on the horizon. Then the plan became no plan at all: sit on the beach and relax.

Ships count as planes-adjacent. Big machines, going somewhere. Same joy.
